# Study Session - 2026-08-20

## Topic
How to evaluate a claimed LLM limitation.

## Main concept
A single failure is an observation, not automatically a general limitation.

Scientific claims require:

- controlled experiments
- clear scope
- alternative explanations
- later validation or disagreement

## Papers studied

Planned historical sequence:

- Brown et al. 2020 - Language Models are Few-Shot Learners
- Bender et al. 2021 - On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots

## Key memory

Capability improvement and limitation removal are not the same thing.
