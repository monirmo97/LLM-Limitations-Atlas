# Inclusion and Exclusion Rules

## Include

A paper is included when it provides at least one of the following about an LLM or closely relevant precursor/foundation language model:

- direct empirical evidence of a limitation or failure mode;
- theoretical argument about a limitation;
- benchmark/evaluation exposing a limitation;
- survey/taxonomy of limitations;
- replication, challenge, or counter-evidence for a limitation claim;
- mitigation paper whose experiments materially characterize the underlying limitation;
- system/safety/evaluation work that reveals a limitation relevant to real LLM behavior.

## Exclude from the deep-study queue but keep in the census when useful

- papers mentioning a limitation only in passing;
- application papers where the limitation is incidental;
- mitigation-only papers that add no evidence about the failure itself;
- duplicate preprint/published records.

## Exclude from the census

- work unrelated to language/foundation models;
- non-scholarly commentary unless used only as context;
- papers outside the target date interval unless needed as pre-2020 historical background.

## Deep-read priority

A paper becomes a core deep read when it is one or more of:

- foundational;
- highly influential;
- methodologically strong;
- representative of a major limitation;
- contradictory to an influential claim;
- a replication;
- a broad survey/taxonomy;
- a frontier 2025–2026 result materially changing the picture.
