# Daily 60-Minute Session

## 00–10 min — Retrieval practice

Explain yesterday's concept and paper from memory before checking notes.

## 10–20 min — Concept lesson

Define today's concept, why it matters, and how it relates to the atlas.

## 20–45 min — Paper study

Read the selected paper with emphasis on research question, method, evidence, and limitations.

## 45–55 min — Teach-back

Explain in your own words:

1. What problem did the paper study?
2. What did the experiment do?
3. What did it find?
4. What does the result prove?
5. What does it not prove?

## 55–60 min — Atlas update

Prepare/update the structured paper note, daily log, category timeline, and progress tracker.
