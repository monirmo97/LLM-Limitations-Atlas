# Curriculum

## Learning objective

Develop a structured mental model of LLM limitations and the evidence behind them.

## Learning sequence

### Module 0 — How to evaluate a limitation claim

Learn to separate:

- observed failure;
- claimed explanation;
- experimental design;
- robustness of the finding;
- scope of the conclusion;
- alternative explanations.

### Module 1 — Foundations (2020–2021)

Study the transition from large pretrained language models to modern LLMs and the early evidence of limitations.

### Modules 2–21 — Limitation categories

One module per category in `TAXONOMY.md`, beginning with reasoning.

Each module contains:

1. definition and boundaries;
2. historical timeline;
3. foundational paper(s);
4. strongest empirical evidence;
5. strongest counter-evidence or qualification;
6. frontier 2025–2026 work;
7. synthesis;
8. recall test.

### Final module — Integrated mental map

Explain relations among limitations, distinguish similar concepts, and analyze a new paper without relying on the taxonomy notes.
