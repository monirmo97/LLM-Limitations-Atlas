# LLM Limitations Taxonomy

Initial working taxonomy.

## A. Core capability limitations

1. Reasoning and problem solving
2. Generalization and compositionality
3. Factuality and hallucination
4. Knowledge boundaries
5. Context and memory
6. Uncertainty, calibration and self-verification
7. Prompt sensitivity and instruction following
8. Grounding, causal and world understanding
9. Multilingual and cultural limitations
10. Multimodal limitations

## B. Learning and control limitations

11. Alignment and controllability
12. Knowledge editing
13. Forgetting and unlearning
14. Agentic planning and tool-use reliability

## C. Safety and societal limitations

15. Bias and fairness
16. Security, jailbreaks and adversarial attacks
17. Privacy, memorization and data leakage

## D. System and evaluation limitations

18. Computational cost and efficiency
19. Benchmark contamination
20. Evaluation validity

This taxonomy remains a research hypothesis and will be updated based on evidence from the literature census.
