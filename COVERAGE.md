# Literature Coverage

## Target interval

- Start: 2020-01-01
- End: 2026-12-31 (project period; current census must record its actual retrieval date)

## Coverage principle

Absolute completeness cannot be assumed. Coverage must be demonstrated through a reproducible search and reconciliation process.

## Planned scholarly sources

- arXiv
- ACL Anthology
- OpenAlex
- Crossref
- Venue-specific proceedings when needed
- Citation chaining from foundational and survey papers

## Coverage checks

For every census release, record:

- retrieval date;
- sources queried;
- exact query or retrieval logic;
- date ranges;
- total records retrieved;
- duplicates merged;
- records excluded and reason;
- records awaiting manual classification;
- known source gaps;
- comparison against major surveys and curated bibliographies.
